# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone contributing to TouchLink.

## Standards

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and communicate clearly.
- Focus on evidence and project goals.

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers and will be reviewed promptly. We reserve the right to remove or reject contributions that do not align with this code of conduct.
